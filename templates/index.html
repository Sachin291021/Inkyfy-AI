About Inkify AI

Inkify AI is an online handwriting converter for turning digital documents into natural-looking handwritten pages. It supports Word documents, PDFs and images, customizable pen colours and paper styles, live preview, and personal handwriting profiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What files can I convert?

You can upload DOCX, PDF, PNG or JPG files. The converter extracts the text and renders it as handwritten pages.

Can I use my own handwriting?

Yes. Use the My Own Handwriting section to create and save a handwriting profile. A processed profile can then be selected during conversion.

Can I download the result?

Yes. After conversion, Inkify AI provides the handwritten document as a PDF download.
